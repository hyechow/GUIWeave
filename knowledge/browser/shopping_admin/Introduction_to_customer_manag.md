# Introduction to customer management

The _Customers_ menu provides access to customer account management tools and gives you the ability to see who is online in your store.

### Adobe Commerce

### Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service

## Display the Customers menu

On the _Admin_ sidebar, click Customers to display the menu options:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| All Customers | Lists all customers who have registered for an account with your store or were added by the administrator. |
| Now Online | Lists all customers and visitors who are currently online in your store. |
| Segments | Lists the customer segments that are used to dynamically display content and promotions to specific customers based on various properties. |
| Customer Groups | The customer groups determine which discounts are available to shoppers and the tax class for the purchase. |
| Companies | (Requires Adobe Commerce B2B) Lists all active company accounts and pending requests, regardless of status setting, and provides the tools used to create and manage company accounts. |
